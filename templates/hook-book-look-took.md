# Template: Hook – Book – Look – Took

Moves from the listener's world into the text and back out to a response. Suits evangelistic sermons and non-christian or mixed audiences. Each `sections:` entry is a `##` heading in the sermon file, spelled exactly as listed; `sections_es:` gives the Spanish headings in the same order. Same length target and file format as the default (`templates/sermon-output.md`).

## Hook

~10–15%. A question, story or tension from everyday life that the passage speaks to. No insider vocabulary (`audiences.md`). End with a bridge: "an old text speaks to exactly this".

## Book

~35%. What the text says. Setting in one or two sentences — who, where (name the place) and when (`audiences.md`) — then the passage in quote blocks (`lookup.py verse`), each with `Literal:` and `Natural:`. Explain the key words from usage evidence; show options on any contested reading; name a variant if it touches familiar wording.

## Look

~30%. What it means for us: the bridge from their situation to ours. One clear idea, drawn from the passage's own words. Answer the obvious "so what?" or objection.

## Took

~15–20%. What to take home: one concrete response worded from the passage's verbs. Evangelistic: a clear, unpressured invitation to respond. Mixed audience: one invitation for those exploring, one for those already following.

## Notes

Not preached. Variants, contested readings with options, translation-comparison findings with class, lookup commands used. Excluded from the spoken-length estimate.
