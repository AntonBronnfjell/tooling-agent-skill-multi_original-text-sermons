# Contested readings

Places where the text honestly allows more than one reading — grammatical, lexical, or textual. When a sermon quotes one of these refs, it must show the original, **each** option with its basis, and what the immediate context favors, stated as evidence ("the data shows…"), never as a verdict on doctrine. Never pick silently, in either direction. `check_sermon.py` warns when a ref below is quoted without an options note.

The `ref` column is machine-read (OSIS, KJV versification). Options are listed in no order of preference. Every original-language form here is copied from `lookup.py` output (commands at the end).

Colwell's rule only says definite predicates before the verb *usually* lack the article; it does not prove definiteness. Show all three. |
